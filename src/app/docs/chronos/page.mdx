# Chronos Time Machine & Executive Cortex

Chronos is the system state archival mechanism within Oasis-Shell. It acts as a DVR for your operating system's exact workflow state.

## State Snapshots

Periodically, or when manually triggered, Oasis-Shell captures a **Chronos Snapshot** and archives it in the SQLite database (`chronos_history`). A snapshot contains:
- **Active Windows**: The exact X/Y coordinates, width, height, and title of every open window.
- **Venture Metrics**: Hardware consumption and project activity levels.
- **Market Intelligence**: Background polling of tech momentum (BTC/ETH prices, sentiment).

## Context Restoration & Crate Naming

When you save a workspace, Oasis-Shell passes the window titles to your local LLM (e.g., `gemma4`) to automatically generate a punchy, 3-4 word title for your context (e.g., *"Market Research Pulse"* or *"Figma UI & React Dev"*). 

When you restore a "Context Crate", Oasis-Shell doesn't just open files. It interacts natively with the OS:
1. **Process Spawning**: It physically spawns the executables of your saved apps, deliberately filtering out core OS dependencies (like `explorer.exe` or `SearchHost.exe`) to prevent system crashes.
2. **Win32 Pixel-Perfect Bounds**: A background thread polls the newly opened windows via `EnumWindows`, hooks into them, and issues `SetWindowPos` to force the apps back into the exact pixel-perfect X/Y coordinates and Width/Height bounds they were in when you saved the crate.

### Neural Intent Commands (`Ctrl + K`)

#### Executive & Cortex Analytics
- **`arr`** / **`runway`** / **`metrics`**: Triggers an Executive Metrics Audit to give you an overview of the current simulated or active venture metrics (e.g., Burn Rate, Runway).
- **`intel`** / **`market`** / **`competitors`**: Initiates a sync with the Market Intelligence Bridge to provide a readout of global tech/AI market momentum.
- **`graph`** / **`cortex`** / **`3d`**: Launches the 3D Strategic Cortex visualization, mapping your tasks, modules, and vaults in an interactive 3D Force Graph.
- **`deploy`**: Initiates the Deployment Sentinel to sync the Edge Cluster or push the current venture to production.
